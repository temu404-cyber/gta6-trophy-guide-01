**Leonida Trophy Guide** — a free offline web app that guides and tracks all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, plus a guide written in our own words that cites its sources (PowerPyx, PlanetGTA6, Exophase, PSNProfiles). The checklist saves progress in the browser, with export and import, search, filters by grade and kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, exploration, combat), and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned. A roadmap view lays out the road to the platinum in four stages — story first (protecting the one possibly-missable meditation in Chapter 1), side strands and activities, collectibles and grinds, then the 100% Completion checklist — with the headline facts (~200 hours, 0 online trophies, 1 playthrough, cheats disable trophies). Two trophies were still unconfirmed when the game launched on 19 November 2026, and their guides say so honestly instead of inventing a method. Everything runs from static files with the network off: no Rockstar logo or artwork, no external requests.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Or try the app live first: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
