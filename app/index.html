    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate at every spot across Leonida — and the first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, can only be done during Chapter 1.       Before you start

      • One playthrough, no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (the game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave slot) — PowerPyx.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Do not use any cheat until the platinum is earned — PowerPyx.
      • Nothing else is missable. Every trophy-related quest giver returns after the story, and the 100% Completion checklist only opens after the story — PowerPyx.
      • Two trophies were unconfirmed at launch (“Bad Luck, I Guess” and “Ten Pin”). Their guides say so honestly instead of inventing a method — PowerPyx.
